Your day on ZeroHero

One battery, four moments a day

All times are your local time. Your battery fills up on cheap or free power, then looks after your home.

12pm – 3pmFree grid powerCharge your battery and run big appliances for free.
3pm – 4pmSolar + batteryYour solar and battery quietly cover the home — nothing to do.
4pm – 11pmBattery power (peak)Grid power costs the most now — this is when your battery earns you money.
11pm – 12pm next dayBattery power (overnight)Your battery carries the home until solar kicks back in.

Tip: keep an eye on your battery's output limit (kW) — if several big appliances run together, the extra may still come from the grid.

Saving 1 · Every day

3 hours of free power

12 PM – 3 PMlocal time, daily

  • Your battery usually charges from solar only. If solar isn't enough, use this window to top it up from the grid for free.

  • Run the washing machine, dryer, dishwasher, hot water, pool pump or charge an EV now — any weather.

  • Free up to 50 kWh a day. A small cost applies after that.

Free up to 50 kWh per day
Saving 2 · Every day

Earn a $1 credit a day

6 PM – 9 PMstay off the grid

  • If your battery covers your total home usage from 6pm–9pm (self-consumption mode), we pay you $1 a day — up to $365 a year.

  • A great way to help offset your daily supply charge.

  • A tiny trickle from the grid is fine — up to 0.03 kWh each hour.

  • Check your battery's app for its output (kW) — too many big appliances at once can pull extra from the grid.

Earn up to $365 a year

Small bonus

Critical Events (optional)

On rare days when power prices spike, our app may ask you to sell or charge for about 5 minutes.

  • Sell during the event: earn $1 per kWh.

  • Charge during the event: earn 5c per kWh.

  • It's completely optional — ignore the message and nothing happens.

Only available if you use GloBird's app to manage your battery — see the next slide.
